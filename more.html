Frequently Asked Questions

How do I join an event?

Simply go to the Search page, look for an event near your city, click "View Details", and view the details to participate. No prior signup is required for public events.

Is the platform completely free?

Yes! [Events] is free to use. All listed community gatherings are open and free to participate unless specified otherwise by the event host.

How do I host my own event?

Go to the About page and check out how to create events, or share details with your community. Hosts are verified to keep meetups safe.
